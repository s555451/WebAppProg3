Superhero Team: Outstanding Eagle and Orchid

"Outstanding Eagle and Orchid" is a crime-fighting duo! Outstanding Eagle possesses supreme aerial vision and swift agility to protect the skies, while sidekick Orchid uses nature's power to create plant shields and heal allies.

Nemesis List & Powers

  • Dr. Chrono-Gloom
    • Major Superpower: Reverses time by 2 minutes to undo tactical mistakes.
    • Minor Superpower: Spawns glowing blue butterflies when speaking.
  • Shadow Viper
    • Major Superpower: Phases through solid concrete walls effortlessly.
    • Minor Superpower: Changes room light bulb colors to purple.
